The production integration at a2516896d535b31403acc0518caa3061e6a08977 failed its first real-B64 exact gate with a device ScatterGather index-out-of-bounds assertion. No successful check or timing result preceded the failure. Original driver f26ea29e8497c9a725bf18c710f0fe04c352c639552323b7d2cc46bd44a1fe71 and frame-pipeline-production-validation.json/log remain unchanged.

The production callback allocated partial_frame on the main capture stream and used it on the auxiliary backbone stream. Its local reference died when the callback returned, before the auxiliary join; main-stream tower-tail allocations could then reuse its storage. The original passing two-frame artifact kept partial_frame as a forward-local until after the join. This is an allocation-lifetime dependency in addition to the explicit CUDA event dependency.

A separate artifact subclass changed only the retention of partial_frame as an attribute before auxiliary launch. Frozen production source remained clean. No timing was collected. Evidence: diagnose_pipeline_lifetime.py, pipeline-lifetime-experiment.json, pipeline-lifetime-command.json, pipeline-lifetime-diagnostic.json/log.
